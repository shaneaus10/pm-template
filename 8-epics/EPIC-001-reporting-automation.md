## Outcome

Leadership receives an accurate weekly status picture that assembles itself, and the [P-001] Operations Lead is out of the manual-reporting business. "Done" = the weekly report is produced from a single reconciled source, approved in under 15 minutes, and never ships stale for a full quarter.

## Bundled Features

- [F-001] Automated Status Digest — the first and (in this example) only feature in scope.

In a real epic you would bundle related features here — e.g. a blocker-escalation alert, a leadership dashboard — that together complete the "reporting automation" outcome.

## Success Metrics

Tied to [VP-001]:

- Weekly report assembly time: 4 hrs → under 15 min.
- Stale/incorrect reports: ~3/month → 0/month.
- Sustained for one full quarter before the epic is considered delivered.
