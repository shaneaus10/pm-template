## Description

The Operations Lead runs day-to-day delivery across the organization's teams. They are accountable for making sure work moves, nothing stalls silently, and leadership has an accurate picture of status each week. They are hands-on with spreadsheets and email but are not a developer.

## Goals

- Keep an accurate, current view of what every team is working on.
- Surface blockers early — before they turn into missed commitments.
- Spend less time assembling reports and more time removing obstacles.

## Pain Points

- Assembling the weekly status report is manual, repetitive, and eats hours.
- Status data is scattered across spreadsheets, chat threads, and inboxes.
- By the time the report is compiled, some of it is already stale.

## Current Workflow Context

Every Friday the Operations Lead pings each team, copies updates into a master spreadsheet, reconciles conflicting numbers, and hand-formats a summary email to leadership. Errors creep in from copy/paste and from teams reporting in different formats.

## Key Workflows

1. Collect status updates from each team.
2. Reconcile and consolidate into a single view.
3. Produce and distribute the weekly leadership summary.
