# Team Discovery Questionnaire

> Use this to interview a team or role before drafting their personas, processes, and value props. The `pm-onboard` skill runs a lighter version of this conversationally; this longer form is useful for a deeper, one-team-at-a-time discovery. Capture answers as notes, then turn them into artifacts.

**Team / role:** ______________________
**Interviewee(s):** ______________________
**Date:** ______________________

---

## Role & responsibilities

1. In one or two sentences, what is this role accountable for?
2. What does a typical week look like?
3. What decisions does this role own?

## Workflows (→ Processes)

4. Walk through your main recurring workflows, step by step. Who hands you inputs? Who consumes your outputs?
5. Which steps are manual, repetitive, or done in a spreadsheet/inbox?
6. Where do errors or delays most often happen?

## Pain & goals (→ Value Props)

7. If you could wave a wand and fix one thing, what would it be?
8. Where do you lose the most time?
9. What would let you spend more time on higher-value work?

## Tools (→ Tool decisions / ADRs)

10. What tools/systems do you use daily? Which are load-bearing?
11. Anything you're forced to use that fights you?

## Rules & constraints (→ Policies / NFRs)

12. What must always be true? (approvals required, data that can't be wrong, compliance/safety rules)
13. What would be unacceptable for an automation to do without a human checking?

## Wrap-up

14. Who else should we talk to?
15. What did we not ask that we should have?
