# SOP — [Process Name]

> A Standard Operating Procedure captures the **runnable, step-by-step** version of a process for a human to follow. It complements a Process artifact `[PROC-NNN]`: the process describes the current-state workflow at a governance level; the SOP is the detailed checklist someone actually executes. Keep a manual SOP runnable as a fallback when a process is automated, so you can roll back if the automation fails.
